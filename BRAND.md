# re.mecch — Brand Guidelines

> **Engineering Beyond Textbooks**
> Instagram: [@re.mecch](https://instagram.com/re.mecch)
> Category: Mechanical Engineering · Design Engineering · Manufacturing · CAD

---

## 1. Brand Identity

re.mecch is a mechanical engineering content brand that makes engineering **practical, understandable, engaging and relatable**.

It bridges the gap between what engineers learn in textbooks and what they actually run into on the shop floor, in design reviews and in industry.

**Philosophy**

> Engineering isn't just about designing things. It's about understanding **why** things work, **how** they are made, and **what happens when things go wrong**.

**Vision:** Make re.mecch the destination for people who love understanding how engineering actually works.

## 3. Personality & Voice

re.mecch sounds like **the senior engineer everyone wants to sit next to** — knows their stuff, explains it simply, and roasts bad design without mercy.

| We are | We are not |
|--------|-----------|
| Technically sharp, approachable | Condescending or gatekeeping |
| Educational, conversational | A classroom lecture or textbook read-aloud |
| Humorous, sarcastic, occasionally provocative | Mean to people, or edgy at the cost of accuracy |
| Curious and experimental | Repeating the same "10 facts" every page posts |
| Practical | Theory for theory's sake |

### Language

- **Hinglish: ~70% Hindi, ~30% English.**
- Keep **technical terms in English** wherever accuracy needs it: *tolerance, fillet, stress concentration, fatigue, feed rate, datum, interference fit*. Never force-translate these.
- Write the way engineers actually talk — e.g.
  - ✅ "Bhai, sharp internal corner draw karna easy hai. Mill karna? Impossible. End mill gol hota hai."
  - ❌ "Aantarik teekshna kon ka nirmaan sambhav nahi hai."
  - ❌ "In this video we will learn about the limitations of milling internal corners."
- For international reach, on-screen text and captions can carry key terms/one-liners in English so the core idea survives without audio.

## 4. Content Style

- **Format:** Short-form Instagram Reels, typically **30–90 seconds**.
- **Hook within the first 3 seconds** — visual + spoken + on-screen text together.
- Visual storytelling: demos, real parts, CAD screen recordings, engineering animations.
- Movie references, memes and relatable situations — used to **carry** the concept, not replace it.
- Every technical claim backed by a real example, part, or failure.
- High-quality visuals with strong **orange–blue contrast**.

**Goal of every piece:** stop the scroll → teach one useful thing → make them remember re.mecch.

### Hook patterns that fit the brand

1. **Contradiction:** "Tumhara design perfect hai. Phir bhi machinist hasega. Kyun?"
2. **Costly mistake:** "Is ek dimension ne company ka ₹2 lakh ka loss karwaya."
3. **Visual mystery:** show the weird part / failure first, explain later.
4. **Textbook vs. reality:** "College mein sikhaya X. Industry mein hota hai Y."
5. **Challenge the viewer:** "Ye drawing mein 3 galtiyan hain. Kitni dikhi?"
6. **POV / relatable:** "POV: Tumne 0.001 tolerance di aur vendor ne quotation bheja."

### Retention & growth checklist

- [ ] Hook in ≤3 s (spoken + text + visual)
- [ ] One core idea per reel — no info dumps
- [ ] Pattern interrupt every 3–5 s (cut, zoom, B-roll, text pop)
- [ ] Payoff delivered before the last 10% (don't make people wait till the end for nothing)
- [ ] Save trigger: something worth coming back to (rule of thumb, table, checklist)
- [ ] Share trigger: "send this to that one friend who…"
- [ ] Follow trigger tied to a series or promise, not generic "follow for more"
- [ ] Keywords naturally in caption + on-screen text (e.g. *GD&T, CNC machining, SolidWorks, DFM*)

## 5. Visual Identity

**Core contrast:** Orange (energy, heat, manufacturing, sparks) vs. Blue (precision, engineering drawings, CAD, cool steel).

Proposed palette — finalize once the logo is locked:

| Role | Name | Hex |
|------|------|-----|
| Primary | Molten Orange | `#FF6A13` |
| Secondary | Blueprint Blue | `#0B3D91` |
| Accent | CAD Cyan | `#00B4D8` |
| Dark base | Machine Black | `#111418` |
| Light base | Drawing White | `#F4F6F8` |

**Guidelines**

- Orange = attention (hooks, highlights, warnings, "wrong" call-outs). Blue/cyan = information (dimensions, annotations, "right" call-outs).
- Use a clean, bold sans-serif for on-screen text (e.g. Montserrat / Inter Bold); a monospace for dimensions and values.
- Keep text inside Instagram safe zones (avoid bottom ~20% and right edge UI).
- Consistent watermark/handle placement on every reel.

## 8. Rules for Anyone (Human or AI) Creating re.mecch Content

1. **Never sacrifice technical accuracy for virality.**
2. Avoid generic engineering facts every page already posts.
3. Prioritize interesting, surprising and practical insights.
4. Use strong hooks and curiosity-driven storytelling.
5. Keep scripts conversational and natural — not AI-sounding.
6. Include visual directions, B-roll suggestions and editing ideas.
7. Design for retention, shareability, saves and follower conversion.
8. Use engineering keywords naturally for discoverability.
9. **Challenge weak content ideas** instead of blindly approving them.
10. Keep the brand personality consistent across all content.
