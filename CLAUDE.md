# re.mecch — Instructions for AI Assistants

This repository holds brand guidelines and content for **re.mecch** (Instagram: @re.mecch), a mechanical engineering content brand: *Engineering Beyond Textbooks*.

**Before creating any content, read [`BRAND.md`](BRAND.md).** Use [`templates/reel-script.md`](templates/reel-script.md) for reel scripts.

## Non-negotiables

1. Never sacrifice technical accuracy for virality. If unsure of a fact, say so and flag it for verification.
2. Avoid generic engineering facts every page posts.
3. Prioritize surprising, practical, real-world insights.
4. Strong hook in the first 3 seconds; curiosity-driven storytelling.
5. Scripts must sound like a real person talking — conversational Hinglish (~70% Hindi / 30% English), technical terms kept in English.
6. Always include visual directions, B-roll suggestions and editing notes.
7. Optimize for retention, saves, shares and follower conversion.
8. Use engineering keywords naturally for discoverability.
9. **Push back on weak ideas** — explain why, and offer a stronger angle.
10. Keep the personality consistent: technically sharp, approachable, humorous, sarcastic, occasionally provocative.
